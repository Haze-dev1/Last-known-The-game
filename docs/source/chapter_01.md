# Chapter 1 — proposed investigation brief

Status: proposal. Character names, chronology, sibling relationship, and final story ending remain unresolved. All new prop text and staging below are proposals, not accepted story canon.

## Arrival and goal

Arrive on foot along a quiet, overgrown Kowloon-inspired street. Reach the family repair shop, recover saved records, and corroborate whether the missing person returned after evacuation. No present-day people, spoken dialogue, or visible protagonist.

## Locations and dependencies

- Street: approach and orientation; short first-person arrival camera sequence, skippable.
- Repair shop: unpowered computer; a portable supply brought on this expedition feeds only the workbench, never the municipal grid.
- Nearby utility point: connect the prepared supply. This reusable action cannot exhaust resources or lock progression.
- Apartment above the shop (later): personal belongings and a proposed acceptance letter establish an independent life.
- Utility room (later): service records and a physical closure notice corroborate the shop archive chronology.

Implemented foundation chain: inspect computer → receive no-power feedback → activate supply → read saved service note → one evidence record. Power can be activated first. Repeated reads leave one record. The saved note proposes charging radios and refers to an upstairs address list; it alone does not establish the author's identity or survival.

Later complete-chapter chain: archive + independently dated utility record + corroborating photograph establish a return after station closure; apartment address list provides an onward station/overflow-shelter lead. Account activity and timestamps are leads until corroborated. Exact dates, addresses, names, photograph content, and storage recovery mechanism require acceptance and research.

## Cutscene triggers and ending

Implemented: a three-second arrival view at first launch; E or Escape skips it. Both paths put the player at the same street position with movement and mouse look enabled.

Proposed later: first restored archive opens a brief first-person examination; corroborated discovery triggers a quiet look toward the apartment; chapter ending follows reading the onward address and reaching the departure point. All remain skippable and return input consistently. No hand animation or video is required.

The eventual chapter answers that the missing person survived evacuation and gives an onward lead. The foundation stops at the first saved clue and allows continued walking; it does not claim to answer that question or finish the chapter.
